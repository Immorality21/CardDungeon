# Playtest notes · 2026-11-21

Raw findings from the owner's playtest. Gathered here first; each fixed one has a **Status** line. The rest gets triaged later into the plan file it belongs to (see `docs/NEXT_STEPS.md`).

## Findings

### 1. The hero's walk looks janky
- **Seen:** when the hero walks through a room (for example the Warrior), the movement feels a bit janky. The sprite seems to change frames at the wrong moments while walking.
- **Cause:** not known yet. Places to look first: tile walking (`Rooms/UI/PartyWalk`, `TileWalkOps`), where the walk frames and the movement along the tiles may not line up.
- **Area:** dungeon rooms / tile walking (`docs/plans/DUNGEON_ROOMS.md`)
- **Status: fixed 2026-11-22, waiting for the owner to try it.** I recorded the walk at 60 fps (position, camera and sprite every frame) and found three causes. (1) The time left over when a step landed was thrown away, and with a held key the next step only began on the following frame, so the party stood still about one frame in eight. The leftover time now carries into the next step (`PartyWalk.Tick`/`Advance`). (2) `SpriteAnimator.Initialize` kept the old frame number and timer, so the cycle played 1, 3, 4 (skipping 2) or held a frame too long. It now starts over, and a turn mid-walk swaps frames in step (`SwapFrames`). (3) The camera followed in `Update`, in no fixed order with the walk; it is in `LateUpdate` now. After the fix every frame moves the same distance and the cycle runs 1-2-3-4. Not changed: the camera looks 1.5 tiles ahead the way the party faces, so it swings across on every turn (playtest 4 findings 18/55). If the walk still feels off, that swing is the next suspect, then the camera's sub-pixel positions (there is no pixel-perfect camera).

### 2. Earth Splitter fires projectiles at the end
- **Seen:** the Warrior's Earth Splitter looks right up to the slam: he jumps and hits the ground. Then it fires a projectile at every enemy, which is wrong.
- **Wanted:** no projectiles. He still jumps and slams the ground, and after a short pause an earth-like sprite or VFX appears on every target at the same moment, as an instant hit.
- **Area:** combat ability visuals (the Earth Splitter ability asset and how its hit is shown; `docs/plans/POLISH_CONTENT.md` battle polish)
- **Same problem:** the Warrior's damage-dealing summon also fires projectiles. It should also hit every target at once with an effect on each target, no projectiles. Fix the two together. Also check whether other hit-all abilities use the projectile path by default.
- **Status: fixed 2026-11-22.** New delivery `MagicDelivery.Ground`: the impact frames play flat under every target at once and the hits land together, no projectile. Earthsplitter uses it with its own slam frames, about 0.3 s after he lands (seen in the Earthsplitter sandbox). The Iron Charger uses it too, borrowing the same frames until it gets art of its own (`SummonSO.Delivery` / `ImpactFrames` are new). Still flying bolts at every enemy: the Galewing and Qyraxis summons, and abilities like Volley, Arrow Storm, Chain Lightning, Deluge, Cluster Bomb, Rivet Storm and Smoke Bomb. Left alone for now; the owner decides which should change.

### 3. Divine Intervention needs an intercept animation
- **Seen:** the Paladin's Divine Intervention now works mechanically, but nothing on screen shows what happened.
- **Wanted:** when it triggers, the Paladin jumps to the middle to "intercept", plays a short animation there, then goes back. That makes it clear, and nice to watch, that he stepped in.
- **Area:** combat visuals (the Paladin's Divine Intervention, the hero's action animations; `docs/plans/POLISH_CONTENT.md` battle polish)
- **Status: done 2026-11-22.** It plays when he casts it, on Earthsplitter's leap: he jumps to the middle of the arena, lands in a guard pose while a pillar of gold light slams down on him and a ring runs out on the ground, the party is covered, and he jumps home. Data only, no code: `UltraSO.LeapSlamFrames` = `Heroes/paladin-intercept.png` (4 frames, 48x48, PixelLab animate_image from his idle frame; 2 generations), `ImpactFrames` = `Ultras/divine-intervention-landing.png` (11 frames, 116x212, ground point at the centre; PixelLab VFX workbench, effect "Divine Intervention Landing"). Seen in `Sandbox/PaladinKit.asset`. It shares the slam's screen shake. Then (owner, same day): **a covered blow flares a gold shield on him** (`SpellVfx.Covered`, played where `ExecuteAttack` redirects the blow; pale blue for an enemy's Guard), and **his Retribution swings back as an attack**. The strike back used to show only a number. It now waits for his flinch to settle, then he lunges at the attacker and the number lands, with the attacker's bar held until then (`ReactionResolved.Strikes`, `CombatManager.PlayStrikeBacks`, `HealthDisplay.HoldAt`). Seen in `Sandbox/PaladinKit.asset`. Not covered: a single-target enemy ability the cover redirects floats no "Cover" and no flash, as before; only basic blows do.

### 4. The item detail panel repeats itself; show the set's pieces instead
- **Seen:** in the hub inventory, the detail panel for an item (screenshot: Shadowweave Cloak, Mage, Chest) lists the same numbers twice. "Grants" shows +2 END, +4 AGI, +18 HP, and "Stats if equipped" shows END +2, AGI +4, HP +18 again. To the owner it is the same information.
- **Wanted:** use that space to show the set's pieces, the way World of Warcraft does in an item tooltip. Show the set name with a count of pieces (for example "Shadowweave (1/4)"), then every piece by name. Pieces the hero is wearing or owns are lit, and missing pieces are greyed out, so you can see what is still needed for the next tier.
- **Open:** is "Stats if equipped" only a duplicate when the slot is empty? It may show a difference against the item already worn (the Padded Jerkin here is worn by the Warrior, not the Mage). Check this before removing it. Also decide whether "owned but not worn" gets its own look.
- **Area:** hub inventory, item detail (`Items/UI/InventoryHubUI`, `ItemSetSO`/`ItemSets`; `docs/plans/POLISH_CONTENT.md` player-facing information)
- **Status: fixed 2026-11-22.** The set section reads "Shadowweave set (1/4)" and lists every piece: worn in gold, owned in plain text, missing greyed out. Each tier is lit once the hero's worn pieces reach it. The open question was answered: "Stats if equipped" compares against the piece already worn, so it stays when the slot is filled and is gone when the slot is empty, because then it only repeated "Grants".
